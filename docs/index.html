Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out between towns.

What stays on your phone

North Dakota Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is North Dakota Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

Three kinds of source. The knoephla and kuchen, steaks and walleye, and oldest-places lists are our own research from fall 2026, with a dated 2025 or 2026 story (such as Tasting Table's) or a visitor bureau's own page behind each place. The James Beard honors are the Foundation's own announcements. The wider directory of 1,736 restaurants starts from open map listings (Overture Maps), filtered to the kinds that proved reliable where states publish restaurant lists.

Where can I get knoephla soup?

Our knoephla list has 6 places, from Kroll's Diner, Tasting Table's pick for North Dakota's best diner, to Wurst Bier Hall in Fargo and Darcy's Cafe in Grand Forks. In the web search, the list puts the nearest first once you share your location.

How do I check a North Dakota restaurant's inspection?

The North Dakota Department of Health and Human Services and eight local health units inspect restaurants, and places on tribal lands are under tribal jurisdiction. Every place links to the department's food and lodging page, and we compute no grade.

Does it show which places serve alcohol?

No. The Attorney General's list of alcohol licensees is a PDF that mixes on- and off-sale licenses, so the app doesn't say either way.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
